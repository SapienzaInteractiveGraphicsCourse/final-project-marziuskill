# Credits & Third-Party Assets

This file documents third-party assets used by **MarziusKill — A Haunted 8-Ball Story**.

The licenses listed below apply to the original source assets. Some audio files are trimmed, normalized, downmixed, reversed, split into one-shots, or otherwise edited for runtime use; those derivatives remain based on the credited source material.

No imported animation clips are used by the project. Imported 3D models are static source assets and are animated, when needed, through JavaScript-controlled scene hierarchies.

### Audio processing

Runtime derivatives were prepared specifically for the game. Processing includes, depending on the source:

- silence trimming and short anti-click fades;
- peak normalization;
- mono downmixing for spatial one-shot effects;
- OGG/Vorbis conversion for browser runtime use;
- extraction of individual footsteps from a long recording;
- extraction of short/medium/long chalk-writing gestures;
- reversing apparition sounds for matching disappearance effects;
- time-compressing the dedicated defeat cue soundtrack while preserving pitch;
- retaining **Moil** as the original stereo MP3 for jukebox playback.

The project audio manifest and source notes preserve the mapping between runtime files and their original recordings.

## Project-Authored Content

The following are implemented by the project rather than imported as third-party animated/gameplay systems:

- billiards physics and rigid-ball simulation;
- cue impact, spin, sliding/rolling behaviour and rail/pocket response;
- ball-return logic;
- trajectory prediction;
- 8-ball rule handling and Solo challenge logic;
- KILL's shot planner and simulator;
- cue hierarchy and all runtime animation;
- pub interaction logic;
- scoreboard rendering;
- pocket, beacon, triangle and prism VFX;
- intro, victory and defeat cinematic sequencing;
- menus, HUD and game-state UI;
- audio orchestration and synchronization.
